Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026

This policy covers text messages sent from the toll-free number (833) 569-9453 (833-JOY-WILD), operated by Ben Wilder for personal use. Messages are sent by Chief, Ben Wilder's personal assistant system.

What messages are sent

Personal and family notifications, such as reminders, schedule updates and alerts, sent only to people who have agreed to receive them (family members, friends and other personal contacts). No marketing or promotional messages are sent.

How consent is collected

Recipients opt in by personally agreeing with Ben Wilder (verbally, in person, or in writing) to receive text messages from this number.

Information collected

We keep only the phone number and name of each opted-in contact and the content of messages needed to send notifications and process replies.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share phone numbers or SMS opt-in data and consent with any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information is shared only with the messaging service provider (Twilio) as needed to deliver messages, or when required by law.

Opting out and help

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.
